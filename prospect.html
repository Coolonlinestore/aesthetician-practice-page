Practice on a prospect

You're making the call. It answers as a real aesthetician business owner — one of several different situations, picked at random, so it won't be the same conversation twice.

It won't hand you the answer

The first answer will be vague on purpose — "it's been slow," that kind of thing. Push past it. Ask what they've already tried, then ask why they think it didn't work.

Want feedback?

Say "how did I do" any time and it'll drop the act and tell you what worked and what to fix.

Allow microphone access when your browser asks. Headphones recommended.
