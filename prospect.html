Practice on a prospect

A prospect has landed on the page and started a conversation. It answers as a real aesthetician business owner — one of five different situations, picked at random, so it won't be the same conversation twice. Keep the script below in front of you.

Your role: You are the one asking the qualifying questions. The AI's role: It plays the business owner who reached out. Your job is to run the qualifying script and learn what's going on in their world before presenting anything.

The script, in order

Step 1 — Opener

Ask about them, not the service

"I'm totally happy to share everything you want to know about the service. Before I do — what's going on in your business right now?"

Even if they open by asking what you do, redirect to their situation first. Then stop talking and let them answer.

Step 2 — Push past the vague answer

Don't accept "business is slow"

"That's interesting, can you tell me a little more about that?"

Keep pushing until it's specific and verifiable — a real number, a real event, a real complaint. Not an "it depends." This is the move most people skip.

Step 3

What have you tried so far?

"What have you tried so far?"

Step 4 — The important one

Why do you think that didn't work?

"Why do you think that didn't work?"

This surfaces what to avoid, and gets them talking themselves into wanting something different — rather than being told.

Step 5 — Before you present anything

Repeat their situation back to them

In their own words. Only then present the plan — short, specific, built entirely out of what they just said. Never a generic pitch.

What you're listening for

  1. Symptoms — what they're dealing with, in their words.
  2. Result — the outcome they're actually trying to create.
  3. The way out — becomes your plan, but only lands if framed against what they've already tried.
  4. What they don't want to do again — comes out of step 4. Your list of things not to propose.

Talk to the prospect

Say "how did I do" at any point and it will drop the act and give you spoken feedback on that round.

Score your round

Tick what you actually did, then save the round. Your history stays on this device so you can see whether you're improving.
