Practice — Qualifying Conversations

Two ways to practice, both out loud. Pick whichever you want to work on. You can stop either one at any time by saying "how did I do" to get feedback.

1. Practice on a prospect

You make the call. It plays a real aesthetician business owner with a real problem — vague at first, so you have to ask properly to get anywhere. Practice your opener and pushing past a thin answer.

2. Get interviewed

The other way around. It runs the qualifying questions on you, so you can feel what a prospect feels being asked them. Good for hearing where the questions land and where they sting.
